Sending a cake to someone in the east

Enter the recipient's address at checkout, and put their name, any delivery instructions and your card wording in the order notes. You can add finishing touches:

  • Greeting card, A$10
  • Gold acrylic cake topper, A$10
  • Helium balloon, A$40

Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we can't guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.

Common questions

Do you deliver cakes to Bondi, Paddington and the Eastern Suburbs?

Yes. The Eastern Suburbs are within our 30 km delivery area. Suburbs such as Paddington, Woollahra, Bondi Junction, Rose Bay and Bondi Beach are roughly 5 to 10 km from Neutral Bay, so delivery is around A$22. Your exact fee is shown in your cart before you pay.

How much is delivery to Randwick, Coogee or Maroubra?

These are around or beyond the 10 km mark from Neutral Bay. Randwick is close to the line between A$22 and A$36, Coogee is around A$36 and Maroubra is A$36. The fee depends on the exact address and is shown before you pay.

Do you deliver to Double Bay and Darling Point?

Yes. Both sit right around the 5 km mark from Neutral Bay, so the fee is A$10 or A$22 depending on the exact address.

Is there a shop in the Eastern Suburbs?

No. We're a home bakery in Neutral Bay, so every cake is baked to order there and then delivered to you. Pickup from Neutral Bay is free.

How far ahead do I need to order?

Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice.
