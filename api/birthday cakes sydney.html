Home-baked birthday cakes in Sydney

Every cake is mixed by hand in small batches in Neutral Bay and baked for your date. Whole cakes start at A$80 and serve 8 to 10 people, and you can have yours picked up from Neutral Bay for free or delivered across Sydney.

  1. Choose your cake. From The Original sticky date to Gooey Chocolate, Tropical Hummingbird and more.
  2. Add finishing touches. A topper, card, balloon or flowers, and your message in the order notes.
  3. Pick the date. Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours’ notice.
  4. Pickup or delivery. Pickup is free. Delivery starts at A$10 and the exact fee is shown before you pay.

Common questions

Can I order a birthday cake for next day?

Yes, if you order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. We do not offer same-day delivery.

How many people does a cake serve?

Our whole cakes serve 8 to 10 people. For a larger group, you can order more than one cake or add Dinner Party Desserts.

Can I add a candle, topper or card?

Yes. From Finishing Touches you can add a gold acrylic cake topper (A$10), a greeting card (A$10), a helium balloon (A$40), flowers (A$50 small or A$100 large) and disposable plates, forks, spoons and napkins (A$20). Put your message wording in the order notes.

Can you make a custom birthday cake?

Yes, please send us an enquiry with your idea. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice.

Can I send a birthday cake to someone else?

Yes. Enter the recipient's address as the delivery address, and put their name, any delivery instructions and your message in the order notes. We deliver within 30 km of Neutral Bay, and the fee is shown before you pay.

Do you have gluten-free or dairy-free birthday cakes?

Gluten Free and Dairy Free options are available on our whole cakes. Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we cannot guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.
