Common questions

What can I order for clients or staff?

The Gift Box (A$15) is one slice of cake with a side of sauce and fresh cream. Mini Sticky Date Cakes, Lumberjack Minis and Gooey Chocolate Minis are A$8 each, and Christmas Pudding Minis are A$10 each, with a minimum order of 10 of each. Whole cakes start at A$80 and serve 8 to 10.

Can you deliver to an office?

Yes. Enter the office address as the delivery address and put the recipient's name and any delivery instructions in the order notes. We deliver within 30 km of Neutral Bay, and the fee is shown in your cart before you pay.

How much notice do you need?

Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom orders need at least 72 hours' notice, so please send an enquiry.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Gluten Free and Dairy Free options are available on whole cakes, the Gift Box and Mini Sticky Date Cakes. Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we can't guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.

Can I order for a larger event?

Send us an enquiry with your date, numbers and delivery address, and we will see what we can do.
