Gluten Free and Dairy Free options

If you or someone you're baking for avoids gluten or dairy, choose Gluten Free or Dairy Free from the dropdown on the cake when you order online. The options are available on:

  • Whole cakes, including The Original, Nutty, Spiced and Crumble sticky date cakes
  • Spiced Carrot & Walnut, Gooey Chocolate, Rich White Chocolate & Raspberry, Tropical Hummingbird and The Lumberjack
  • The Gift Box
  • Mini Sticky Date Cakes (not the other minis)

Please note: Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we can't guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup from Neutral Bay is free. Delivery within 30 km starts at A$10 and the exact fee is shown before you pay. Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours’ notice.

Common questions

Which cakes come in a gluten-free option?

Gluten Free and Dairy Free options are available on our whole cakes, the Gift Box and our Mini Sticky Date Cakes. Choose the option from the dropdown when you add the item to your cart.

Do the Lumberjack Minis, Gooey Chocolate Minis or Christmas Pudding Minis come gluten free?

No. The gluten-free and dairy-free options are available on the Mini Sticky Date Cakes only, not on the other minis.

Are your gluten-free cakes safe for coeliacs?

We can't guarantee that. Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we can't guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.

Do you offer dairy-free cakes?

Yes. Dairy Free is an option on our whole cakes, the Gift Box and the Mini Sticky Date Cakes, chosen from the same dropdown.

How far ahead do I need to order?

Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice.
