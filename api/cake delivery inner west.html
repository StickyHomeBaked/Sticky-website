Common questions

Do you deliver cakes to Newtown, Leichhardt and the Inner West?

Yes. The Inner West is within our 30 km delivery area. Suburbs such as Glebe, Annandale, Leichhardt, Newtown, Stanmore and Haberfield are roughly 5 to 10 km from Neutral Bay, so delivery is around A$22. Your exact fee is shown in your cart before you pay.

How much is delivery to Burwood and Strathfield?

Burwood and Strathfield are around 10 to 20 km from Neutral Bay, so delivery is around A$36. The fee for your exact address is shown before you pay.

How much is delivery to Balmain and Rozelle?

These sit close to the 5 km mark from Neutral Bay, so the fee is A$10 or A$22 depending on the exact address.

Can I pick up my cake instead?

Yes. Pickup from Neutral Bay is free, and we confirm your pickup time after you order.

How far ahead do I need to order?

Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice.
