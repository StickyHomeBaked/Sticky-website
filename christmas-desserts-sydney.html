Planning your Christmas order

  • The minimum order is 10 of each dessert.
  • We bake to order in small batches, so please order as early as you can for Christmas week.
  • Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day pickup or delivery. After 3pm, the earliest date is the day after tomorrow. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours’ notice.
  • Pickup from Neutral Bay is free, and delivery within 30 km starts at A$10.
  • For a larger or special request, send us an enquiry.

Common questions

Do you make Christmas puddings?

Yes. Our Christmas Pudding Minis are individual Christmas puddings made for the festive table. They are A$10 each with a minimum order of 10, and they are part of our Dinner Party Desserts.

How many Christmas Pudding Minis do I need to order?

The minimum order is 10 of each dessert. As a simple guide, order one per guest, and add another flavour such as Mini Sticky Date Cakes if you want a choice on the table.

Can I get Christmas desserts delivered in Sydney?

Yes. We deliver within 30 km of Neutral Bay, from A$10, and the fee for your address is shown before you pay. Pickup from Neutral Bay is free.

How far ahead should I order for Christmas?

We bake to order in small batches, so please order as early as you can for Christmas week. Order by 3pm (Sydney time) for next-day orders. For larger or special requests, send us an enquiry.

Do you have gluten-free or dairy-free Christmas desserts?

Gluten Free and Dairy Free options are available on our Mini Sticky Date Cakes, but not on the Christmas Pudding Minis. Our cakes are made in a home kitchen where gluten, dairy, nuts and other allergens are used, so we can't guarantee any cake is completely free of allergens. Please tell us about any allergies in your order notes.
